Charity events that bring our city together

"We believe a community changes when its people show up. Every fun run, gala and concert on this site is a chance to turn an ordinary weekend into something that matters."

Giving Coast is the events platform of four Gold Coast charities. We list every fundraiser they run, so you can find a cause you care about, see exactly what it is raising money for, and register in a couple of minutes.

Find an event

Who we are

Four local charities, one shared calendar.

🏠 Our mission

To make giving simple and visible. Every event on this site shows its fundraising goal and how far along it is, so you always know what your ticket is paying for.

🎯 What we fund

Emergency housing, school supplies and tutoring, dune restoration and turtle monitoring, and mobile health clinics serving the coastal towns north of the city.
